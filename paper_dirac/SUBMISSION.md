# Paper A — submission plan

## Target journal (verified 2026-07-11, web-checked)

**Physical Review A (APS) — subscription (non-open-access) track.**
- APC on the subscription track: **$0** (verified on journals.aps.org/authors/apcs).
- Quartile Q1, flagship for relativistic atomic structure / Dirac–Coulomb.
- **At submission choose the subscription / non-OA option.** Do NOT opt
  into gold OA ($2,910, 2026). Do NOT rely on an APC waiver — Ecuador is
  upper-middle income and almost certainly does not qualify.
- Open access is still achieved for free: post the arXiv preprint and the
  GitHub code (already public).

### Ranked alternatives (all free-to-publish on the stated track)
1. **SciPost Physics Core** — diamond OA, free to publish AND free to
   read (CC-BY). The only top option that is both. Fits method + theorem;
   its open-code ethos matches our release. (SciPost Physics proper is
   more selective — needs a stronger impact case.)
2. **Computer Physics Communications** (Elsevier, subscription track $0)
   — best if we foreground the released solver (CPC Program Library).
3. **Journal of Physics B** (IOP, subscription track $0) — direct AMO
   scope, Q2, solid fallback.
4. **Communications in Computational Physics** (Global Science Press,
   global-sci.com — verify the domain) — computational-methods fit.

### Avoid
- Chinese Physics C — wrong scope (HEP; SCOAP3 free-OA is HEP-only).
- globalscienceresearchjournals.org — predatory look-alike of the
  legitimate global-sci.com. Verify the domain before any submission.
- Science Publishing Group and generic pay-to-publish "International
  Journal of …" venues — APC-charging, low rigor.
- Metric-aggregator sites (manusights, letpub, academic-accelerator) are
  NOT authoritative for fees — always confirm on the publisher's page.
